**Why the advisory is wrong:**
The previous approach ran max-flow on capacities u(e) alone, checking if total throughput ≥ D. This fails because it does not verify that mandatory minimum flows l(e) can coexist with the maximum flow. An edge might be forced to carry l(e) = 80 but also be essential for sending additional flow to meet global demand; the unadjusted max-flow might saturate other edges in a way incompatible with the minimums.

**Why the transformation works:**
By pre-allocating l(e) and solving for the *residual* capacity u'(e) = u(e) − l(e), we separate the two problems: (1) Can we satisfy the mandatory minimums? (2) With those minimums fixed, can we satisfy the remaining demand? The saturation condition on step 9 answers both simultaneously—it proves that the mandatory minimums and the full demand can be honored on the same feasible flow.
