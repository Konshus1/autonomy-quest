A review submission is `{id,candidate,department,decision,scope,comment,supersedes}` with decision `approve|reject|comment`; scope is a sorted list of region names, and supersedes is a prior review ID or null. An approval remains usable on a later candidate for the regions in its scope that are byte-for-byte unchanged through the supplied candidate lineage, and becomes stale only for scoped regions that changed. Each required department needs usable approval coverage of every required region. Blockers use `DEPT missing approval`, `DEPT rejected: COMMENT`, or `DEPT approval lacks REGION`; stale entries use `REVIEW stale: REGION`. `ancestry` is a sorted list with one plain-language line per current region: `REGION: introduced in ID`, `REGION: unchanged from ID through ID`, or `REGION: changed in ID (previous ID)`. Each `ZipInfo.date_time` is `(1980,1,1,0,0,0)` and `create_system` is 0 and `external_attr` is `0o600 << 16`. `checksums.json` maps the other three names to SHA-256 digests.
