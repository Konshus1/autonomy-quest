It must support beginning a session, recording contact confirmation, choosing an appointment, correcting prior input, and completing or abandoning check-in. Public API and data-format contract (frozen):

Public `session` is a dictionary with exactly `active`, `contact_confirmed`, `appointment_id`, and `completed`, initially `False`, `None`, `None`, `False`. `handle(action) -> dict` accepts a dictionary whose `type` is one of `begin`, `confirm_contact`, `choose_appointment`, `correct`, `complete`, or `abandon`. After a choice it is `Review and complete`; after completion `Check-in complete`; after abandon `Session abandoned`. Appointment choices are present exactly when the prompt is `Choose appointment`, otherwise empty. Accepted screens have an empty messages list except completion yields `["Checked in"]` and abandon yields `["Check-in abandoned"]`.

`run_once()` requires the supplied source and sink, obtains one action by calling `action_source.read_action()`, passes it to `handle`, calls `screen_sink.display(screen)` exactly once, and returns that same screen object. Missing source or sink raises `RuntimeError` before either is called.
