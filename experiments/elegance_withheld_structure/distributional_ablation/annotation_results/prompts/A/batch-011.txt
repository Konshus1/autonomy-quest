Public API and data-format contract (frozen):

Public `session` is a dictionary with exactly `active`, `contact_confirmed`, `appointment_id`, and `completed`, initially `False`, `None`, `None`, `False`. `handle(action) -> dict` accepts a dictionary whose `type` is one of `begin`, `confirm_contact`, `choose_appointment`, `correct`, `complete`, or `abandon`. A screen dictionary has exactly `prompt: str`, `messages: list[str]`, and `choices: list[dict]`; choices use `{"id": str, "label": str}` in appointment input order. `begin` is accepted only when inactive and starts a clean session. Contact and appointment actions require an active, incomplete session. `complete` additionally requires confirmed contact and a selected appointment; it marks completed and inactive. `abandon` requires an active session and resets to the initial session. After an accepted begin/contact/correction the prompt is respectively `Confirm contact`, `Choose appointment` (if confirmed, otherwise `Confirm contact`), or the next unmet prompt. Appointment choices are present exactly when the prompt is `Choose appointment`, otherwise empty.
