Actual minutes run from the first proceeding for an item until the next proceeding for a different item or the last proceeding, less recess overlap; an item with none has zero. Errors are sorted and include all applicable `unknown item X`, `timestamps out of order`, `recess ends before it starts`, and `invalid reorder`; invalid calls do not alter the five document views.
