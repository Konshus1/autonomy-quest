At an architecture firm, a compromised laptop is encrypting shared files. The company network permits direct peer-to-peer connections among employee machines. The incident log shows that the encryption process copied itself from the infected laptop to three peer machines using those connections. Those same peer connections can reach two still-uninfected computers that run payroll. The team's stated goal is to stop further spread and protect payroll, but payroll must remain available. They tried inspecting and cleaning infected hosts one at a time. During each inspection another peer was compromised, falsifying the assumption that host-by-host cleanup would be faster than the spread. No response action has yet been approved.
