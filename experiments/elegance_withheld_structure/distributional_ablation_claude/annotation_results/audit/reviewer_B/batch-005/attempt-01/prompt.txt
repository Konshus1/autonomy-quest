It must support beginning a session, recording contact confirmation, choosing an appointment, correcting prior input, and completing or abandoning check-in. Public API and data-format contract (frozen):
The module must export `CheckInConsole(appointments, action_source=None, screen_sink=None)`. `appointments` is a sequence of dictionaries with unique string `id` and string `label`; it is copied. Public `session` is a dictionary with exactly `active`, `contact_confirmed`, `appointment_id`, and `completed`, initially `False`, `None`, `None`, `False`. `handle(action) -> dict` accepts a dictionary whose `type` is one of `begin`, `confirm_contact`, `choose_appointment`, `correct`, `complete`, or `abandon`. The latter fields are: `confirmed: bool` for contact, `appointment_id: str` for choice, and `field` plus `value` for correction, where field is `contact_confirmed` (boolean value) or `appointment_id` (known id or None).

A screen dictionary has exactly `prompt: str`, `messages: list[str]`, and `choices: list[dict]`; choices use `{"id": str, "label": str}` in appointment input order. `begin` is accepted only when inactive and starts a clean session. After an accepted begin/contact/correction the prompt is respectively `Confirm contact`, `Choose appointment` (if confirmed, otherwise `Confirm contact`), or the next unmet prompt. After a choice it is `Review and complete`; after completion `Check-in complete`; after abandon `Session abandoned`.
