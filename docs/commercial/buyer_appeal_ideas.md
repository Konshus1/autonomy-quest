# Buyer appeal + quality ideas (6087 / #4834)

Draft. Not a post. Not a promise we have not earned.

Two missions sit on this work. **#4834** is: a fleet that gets better at missions by testing hypotheses and keeping only what is true. **The product (Agentricity)** is: a human with a day job hands it a measurable goal; it works; it will not lie about the score.

People with money do not buy architecture. They buy a Tuesday that hurts less. The beachhead is a solo founder, tiny team, or small agency who still does their own follow-up at 11pm and got burned by “AI employees” that spammed their list.

## What must stay true

- Receipts, not claims (2632). No “flagship works,” no “learns autonomously,” no revived analogy cost-lever.
- Public/try is safe because it **cannot** do the dangerous things. Paid is safe because the **brakes are enforced**.
- Kevin cannot take sales calls. Every idea below is self-serve or async email.
- “Runs your business” is a burned phrase (2024–25 AI-SDR bust). Keep the outcome; change the words.

## Quality ideas (make the thing more worth buying)

1. **One human job, end to end, as the flagship dogfood.**  
   Not “operate marketing.” Pick a job a paying human already hates: *who is waiting on me*, *stale catalog rows*, *weekly status I never write*. Run it ourselves. Publish the real number, including weeks it moved zero.

2. **First-48-hours artifact.**  
   Cold start is the product-quality hole. A new buyer must get one useful, honest artifact in two days (three researched prospects, a ranked follow-up list, a “nothing moved and here is why”). If we cannot do that, $299 is rent on a science project.

3. **Fire-the-gate live.**  
   A 20-second clip: operator closes `contact_human`, the next planned send parks. That is quality *and* the purchase reason for anyone who has money on the line.

4. **Scoreboard vs. story, side by side.**  
   Every mission page shows the DB-read number and the agent’s last sentence. If they diverge, the page says so. This is the anti-Goodhart demo buyers can feel.

5. **Refusal is a first-class product event.**  
   The Cal.com / Formbricks hold is the quality story. Surface refusals in the UI the way we surface wins. A system that hides its stalls will eventually fake.

6. **Agency isolation as a quality feature.**  
   One envelope per client account. A mistake in account A cannot spend or write in account B. That is how you sell to agencies without becoming an SDR blaster.

## Appeal ideas (humans with money, product that helps humans)

### Change the hero from category to Tuesday

Current H1: *“An autonomous operator that runs your business — and does your marketing.”*  
True-ish, burned, founder-facing.

Try (draft only):

- *“The operator that will tell you it’s stuck.”*
- *“Hand it a number you care about. It works the number. It won’t invent the number.”*
- *“For the founder who is still the follow-up department.”*

Value first, honesty as trust, dogfood as proof — same order as the site rubric. Different words.

### Sell three jobs, not a platform

People pay for jobs:

| Job | What they feel | What we can honestly show |
|---|---|---|
| Don’t let leads rot | Sunday dread | Prospect CRM that only contains real companies we researched |
| Don’t lie to myself | Fake dashboards | Measure is a DB read; refusals are logged |
| Don’t let it email my customers | Fear of AI-SDR | Gate closed until they open `contact_human` |

If the site named those three jobs above the fold, a buyer with money would know why to pay. “Autonomous operations” does not do that.

### Price against a human slice, not against “AI”

$299 once / $99/mo is cheap vs a VA or a junior ops person, expensive vs a toy. Say that plainly:

*“Less than one afternoon of a contractor. If it does not produce one honest artifact in the first week, email us — we will refund Own it.”*

Only offer that refund if we can actually honor it. If we cannot, do not write it.

### The Museum is the ad

2632 already wants a Proof Ledger. For buyers, one card beats ten features:

> It researched real companies. It would not mark them paying customers. The number stayed 0.

Caption: *You can trust this scoreboard because it is willing to look bad.*

Later, next to it: *Then it recorded three real prospects and asked to copy what it learned.* That is a second card, not a rewrite of the first.

### Talk to the person, not the evaluator

Evaluators want envelopes and HMAC. Payers want: *Will this take a load off me without humiliating me in front of customers?*

Put the evaluator stuff behind “For the technically curious” (already on the page). Put the human load in the hero.

### One sentence for managed / Stay current

Not “continuously vetted.”  
*“You are busy. We keep the brakes and the updates current so you do not have to become the AI ops person.”*

That is the paid human-help pitch.
