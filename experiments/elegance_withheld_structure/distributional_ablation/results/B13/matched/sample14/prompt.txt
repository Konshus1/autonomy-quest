Public API/data-format contract: `adapt(recipe, request, catalog) -> dict` and `print_original(recipe) -> str`. Quantities and yields are `fractions.Fraction`; other values are JSON-shaped. A recipe has `{title,yield,ingredients,equipment,instructions,components,authored_text}`. Each catalog choice has `{id,for,priority,result}`; `result` describes a replacement ingredient (`name`, and optional `quantity_factor`, `unit`, `preparation`), optional `wording_changes` pairs `{old,new}`, optional equipment additions/removals, and optional additional ingredients. Lower numeric priority wins, with catalog order as the tie-breaker. The returned value is `{possible,recipe,choices,warnings,reasons}`. If possible, `recipe` is a complete adapted recipe at the target yield, all quantities reflect the yield change and selected replacement factors, no excluded ingredient remains anywhere, its equipment is sorted and contained in `available_equipment`, selected wording changes are reflected in the final instruction strings, and `reasons` is empty. `choices` is the ordered list of chosen catalog IDs and `warnings` is sorted. If impossible, `recipe` is null and `reasons` is a sorted list containing every independently applicable reason, not merely the first; reason text is `no substitution for NAME`, `equipment NAME unavailable`, or `substitution cycle involving NAME`. `choices` may describe choices considered before impossibility was established. Additional ingredients are subject to the same exclusions and catalog choices, so chained adaptations are supported. `print_original` returns `authored_text` byte-for-byte as a Python string. Neither function may mutate its inputs.
