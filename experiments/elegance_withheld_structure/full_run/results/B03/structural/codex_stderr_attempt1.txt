Public API and data-format contract (the implementation's internal organization is unrestricted):
Provide `AccessService(requirements)`. Requirements are dictionaries with a unique string `id`, a `type`, and type-specific fields. Supported forms are: `{"type":"badge", "allowed":[...]}`, `{"type":"training", "name":...}` (the request's `training` mapping holds expiry datetimes and the expiry is valid when it is at or after the request timestamp), `{"type":"escort"}`, `{"type":"window", "weekdays":[0..6], "start":"HH:MM", "end":"HH:MM"}` (equal endpoints mean all day; an end earlier than start is overnight and its after-midnight part belongs to the preceding allowed weekday), `{"type":"closure", "start":datetime, "end":datetime}` (inclusive start, exclusive end), and `{"type":"emergency_override", "overrides":[requirement IDs...]}`.
